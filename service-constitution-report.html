What's in your report

Nine parts, in the order you'll use them.

Every report follows the same structure, so you know what you are getting before you start. Each point ties back to something you told us or something the physician saw.

1

At a glance

A short summary up front: the thing that bothers you most, three points on how your body has been running, and the three changes to start with.

2

What your photographs show

What the physician sees in your tongue, face, hands, nails and ears, in everyday words rather than technical terms, and which of your own answers it fits.

3

How your body has been running

Whether you tend to run warm or cold, damp or dry, full or depleted, and how that shows up in your energy, your digestion and your sleep.

4

What in your daily life is shaping it

Your own answers set against that picture: meal times, cold drinks, late nights, long hours sitting. Which habits to keep, and which to stop.

5

Your everyday plan

Up to eight short sections built around your own habits: when to eat, how to build a plate, how to cook and drink, sleep, keeping warm, movement and a steadier mind. Each with a small, specific target, such as dinner finished by seven on weekdays.

6

Your first three changes

In order, with the reason each one comes first, so you know exactly where to begin the day your report arrives.

7

What to keep an eye on

What to notice in your energy, sleep and digestion over the following weeks, and a simple check-in to note it down.

8

When to see your GP

The signs that mean you should book an appointment with your GP rather than wait, written for your own picture. This part is in every report.

9

Your questions

A short closing summary from the physician, then your private WhatsApp group for questions. How that works is in the questions below.

Insight, not treatment

What the report looks at, and what it leaves out.

Your body does a lot of its own upkeep, like a house that tidies itself as it goes. When more comes in than it can clear, day after day, it falls behind and you start to feel it. The report looks at how well your body has been keeping up, and what in your daily life is adding to the load. It doesn't look for disease.

What it does

  • Looks at function and the whole system, rather than one complaint at a time
  • Turns a scatter of "random" symptoms into one story pointing in one direction you can work with
  • Gives you a plan to act on yourself: nothing else has to follow
  • Keeps to everyday things: any ingredients are something you'd source from your own local shops

What it leaves out

  • A medical diagnosis or treatment. It doesn't replace your GP
  • Herbs or prescriptions: nothing is prescribed
  • Advice on starting, changing or stopping any medication. Any change to what you're prescribed is between you and your GP
  • A promise of results. It's a clear place to start, made for you

Good to know

Common questions

What do I do once I have it? Do I need to see a TCM practitioner?

No. The report is written for you to act on yourself. Nothing is prescribed, and nothing else has to follow. If you would later like someone to work with you in person, the Observation Reading is where that starts, but you do not need it to use your report.

Do I need to be ill to get one?

Not at all. You might simply want to understand your energy, sleep or digestion better. The report is written the same way whether you are optimising or "off but fine on paper."

Can I ask questions afterwards?

Yes. When your report arrives, we set you up with a private WhatsApp group, just you and our team, where you can ask about it for 30 days. You can send up to two rounds of questions, and we reply to each within five working days. We can help with anything in the report you'd like explained, how to fit the plan around your work, family, diet or travel, and what you've noticed since you started. We can't comment on new or different symptoms, test results or medication, and we don't diagnose. If something new comes up, or anything gets worse, please see your GP first.

What about the Observation Reading?

It is a separate service for a different need, not an add-on to this report and not the next step after it. The Observation Reading (£850) is a deeper reading of your whole picture by one independent practitioner who reads every case herself. It is also the way in if you want to be seen in person in China.
