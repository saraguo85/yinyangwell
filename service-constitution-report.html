What a Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) Wellness Report is, and isn't

Western tests are brilliant at finding disease: something broken, out of range. They're not built to see how well your body is running when nothing is technically wrong. A TCM Wellness Report looks at something different: at function, at the whole system, and turns a scatter of "random" symptoms into one story pointing in one direction you can work with.

It's not a diagnosis and it's not a cure. It's a clear map of how you're running right now.

It's read by senior, licensed doctors at our partner clinic in China; we coordinate and translate. Any ingredients are something you'd source from your own local shops. If you have a medical condition or concerning symptoms, please see a licensed healthcare provider. A TCM Wellness Report sits alongside that, it doesn't replace it.

Good to know

Common questions

What do I do once I have it? Do I need to see a TCM practitioner?

No. The report is written for you to act on yourself: what in your daily life is shaping how your body has been running, what to stop, what to keep doing, and the changes to make first. Nothing is prescribed, and nothing else has to follow. If you would later like someone to work with you in person, the Observation Reading is where that starts, but you do not need it to use your report.

Do I need to be ill to get one?

Not at all. You might simply want to understand your energy, sleep or digestion better. The report is written the same way whether you are optimising or "off but fine on paper."

Is this a diagnosis?

No. It's a read of your whole picture: insight into how your body is running and what supports it. It isn't medical diagnosis or treatment, and doesn't replace your GP.

What do you need from me?

A short online intake about your symptoms, sleep, digestion, energy and lifestyle, a brief medical history, and clear photos of your tongue, face, hands, nails and ears. You can attach test results or a specialist letter if you have them; nobody needs them to write your report. Ten minutes, no account.

Who actually reads it?

A senior TCM doctor, licensed in China, at Baoputang Clinic, our partner clinic in Ningbo. Not an app, and not left unchecked. We coordinate and translate the write-up for you.

What if I want to go further than this?

The other reading we arrange is the Observation Reading (£850), by one independent practitioner who reads every case herself, working from observation and a full history, which also sets out whether sessions in person would add anything and what that would involve. It is a different reader and a different depth, not an add-on to this report. Most people who buy the £69 report never need it, and we will say so if we think that is you.
