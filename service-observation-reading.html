The report

What is in it, and what is deliberately not.

What the reading contains

  • What she sees in the photographs, described plainly rather than in terminology you would have to look up
  • How your body has been running, in the way TCM reads it: the whole picture rather than a list of complaints
  • Where she thinks this has come from: the living, working and eating that shaped it
  • What to be careful of, and which of your current habits are working against you
  • What she would change first, and in what order: sleep timing, warmth, food, movement, pace
  • Whether she thinks sessions in person would add anything, and honestly how much
  • A short window afterwards to come back with questions on anything unclear

What it is not

  • Not a medical diagnosis, and not a second opinion on one you already have
  • Not a test result, and not something to take to your GP as evidence
  • Not a decision about whether you qualify for sessions in person: that decision is yours
  • Not a herbal prescription; nothing is prescribed, supplied or shipped
  • Not advice on starting, changing or stopping any medication
  • Not a diagnosis of any named disease, growth or abnormality, and it will not tell you that you have one
  • Not a substitute for investigation if you have symptoms that need investigating

Your photographs and health information

Photographs of your face and details of your health are sensitive personal data under UK law. They are used only to produce your reading, shared only with the practitioner who writes it, and never published, used in marketing or shown as an example, including with faces obscured.

Your intake and photographs are collected through our form provider and stored on its servers in the United States. To be read, they have to reach the practitioner in China. They are sent to her as a link that we withdraw afterwards rather than as an attachment, so the copy she works from does not sit in anyone's inbox. You are asked to agree to that transfer separately before you start, because China is not covered by a UK adequacy decision and your rights over the data are harder to enforce there than they are here.

We keep your intake, your photographs and your reading for three years, so that a later reading can be compared against your own history rather than started from nothing, and so that we can answer any question about a reading you have paid for. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time, and we confirm in writing once it is done. Records of your payment are kept for six years, because tax law requires it. If you join our mailing list, your name and email address stay on it until you unsubscribe, which is separate from all of this and never includes anything about your health.

Most of it is yours to act on at home: what she sees, what in your daily life she thinks has shaped it, what to stop, what to keep, and what she would change first.
