The report

What you come away with.

What the reading contains

  • What she sees in the photographs, described plainly rather than in terminology you would have to look up
  • How your body has been running, in the way TCM reads it: the whole picture rather than a list of complaints
  • Where she thinks this has come from: the living, working and eating that shaped it
  • What to be careful of, and which of your current habits are working against you
  • What she would change first, and in what order: sleep timing, warmth, food, movement, pace
  • Whether she thinks sessions in person would add anything, and honestly how much
  • A short window afterwards to come back with questions on anything unclear

What it leaves out

  • A medical diagnosis of any kind, or a second opinion on one you already have
  • Herbs or prescriptions: nothing is prescribed, supplied or shipped
  • Advice on starting, changing or stopping any medication
  • A verdict on whether you qualify for sessions in person: that decision is yours
