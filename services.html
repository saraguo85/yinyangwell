Services

Two readings, and what may follow from them.

Both readings are written assessments, delivered remotely. Neither is a medical diagnosis, and neither requires you to travel. Which one you want depends on how far you intend to take it, and stopping at the first one is a perfectly good outcome.

The usual first step

TCM Wellness Report

A detailed intake — sleep, digestion, energy, temperature, what you have already tried, plus tongue and face photographs, read by a senior physician at Baoputang Clinic, our licensed partner clinic in Ningbo, China. You receive a written account of what they see, how your body has been running in the way Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) reads it, and the everyday changes that shift it: sleep timing, keeping warm, how and what to eat, movement. Practical, and yours to act on straight away.

£69
one-off · written by a clinic physician
What it covers
In depth · by arrangement

Observation Reading

A longer reading by one independent practitioner, working from observation and a full history. It sets out what she sees, where she thinks it has come from, what to be careful of, and what she would change first, and, if treatment in person would add anything, honestly how much. It is finished when the report reaches you; most people stop there. She is not part of the Baoputang team and is not a medical doctor.

£850
written report · limited each month

Being seen in person, in China

The same practitioner sees a small number of people face to face. This is never sold in advance and never as a package: how many sessions would be worth making, and what they would cost, are written into your Observation Reading before you commit to anything. Quoting a figure before anyone has read you would be quoting for work nobody has assessed. We arrange the practitioner's time and an interpreter; flights and accommodation you book yourself, in your own name.

How it works

Three steps, and a stop at every one.

01

You fill in the intake

Online, in your own time: symptoms, history and lifestyle, plus photographs taken in daylight to a short written guide we send you.

02

One person reads it and writes back

The same person who looks at your photographs writes your report. Nothing is triaged by an assistant first.

03

You decide what, if anything, happens next

With the report in front of you. There is no obligation at any point, and we do not follow up to press it.

Honest limits

What none of this is.

  • Not a medical diagnosis, and not a second opinion on one you already have.
  • Not a reason to delay seeing a doctor. If you have symptoms that need investigating, that comes first.
  • Not a herbal prescription. Nothing is prescribed, supplied or shipped.
  • Not advice on medication. We do not advise on starting, changing or stopping anything you have been prescribed.
