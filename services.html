Services

Two readings, and what may follow from them.

Both readings are written assessments, delivered remotely. Neither is a medical diagnosis, and neither requires you to travel. They meet two different needs: the Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) Wellness Report is for everyday changes, and the Observation Reading reads your whole picture in depth and what she thinks has shaped it: complete in itself, and the way in if you want to be seen in person in China.

For everyday changes

TCM Wellness Report

A detailed intake covering sleep, digestion, energy, whether you run cold or hot, what you have already tried, plus tongue and face photographs, read by a senior physician at Baoputang Clinic, our licensed partner clinic in Ningbo, China. You receive a written report of what they see, how your body has been running in the way Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) reads it, and the everyday changes that shift it: sleep timing, keeping warm, how and what to eat, movement. Practical, and yours to act on straight away.

Observation Reading

A deeper reading by one independent practitioner, working from observation and a full history to trace where things began. It sets out what she sees, where she thinks it has come from, what to be careful of, and what she would change first, and, if sessions in person would add anything, honestly how much. It is complete when the report reaches you, and whether to go on to sessions in China is your decision, made after you have read it. She works independently, separate from Baoputang Clinic.

Being seen in person, in China

The same practitioner also sees clients in person at her own practice in China. She sees one client at a time, and each session is an hour. This is never sold in advance and never as a package: how many sessions would be worth making, and what they would cost, are written into your Observation Reading before you commit to anything. We arrange the practitioner's time and an interpreter; flights and accommodation you book yourself, in your own name.

What it involves
Which one

One for daily life, one for the whole picture.

Read down the two lists and see which sounds more like you. If you're still not sure, write to us and we'll tell you which one fits.

The £79 report, if

  • What you want is how your body has been running and what to change day to day
  • You would like to make the changes yourself, at home, at your own pace
  • You feel off rather than ill: low energy, poor sleep, an unsettled stomach, always cold or too hot, a foggy head
  • You are not looking to travel for sessions in person

The £850 reading, if

  • You are thinking about being seen in person in China, and want to know first whether it would add anything
  • You are living with pain: back, neck, shoulder, knee, hip, most often
  • Your tests come back normal, you still do not feel right, and you want to know what she thinks is behind it
  • You have a diagnosis and are under medical care, and want your whole picture read alongside it
  • You would like to talk it through with her live, on video with an interpreter, as well as have it in writing
How it works

Three simple steps.

01

Fill in the form

Online, in your own time: your health, your history and your daily habits, plus a few photos. We send you a short guide on how to take them.

02

Get your reading

For the TCM Wellness Report, a senior physician at Baoputang Clinic reads everything and writes it up for you. For the Observation Reading, the practitioner reads you herself, live on video with an interpreter, then sends it to you in writing.

03

Decide what's next

Make the changes at home, or, after the Observation Reading, think about sessions in China. It's entirely up to you.
