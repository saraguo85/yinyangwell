When the tests say normal, but you don't feel it.

For people with a file of normal results who still don't feel right, and for people living with pain who want it looked at as part of the whole of them. We arrange two readings from China: one by a senior physician at a licensed Traditional Chinese Medicine clinic, one by an independent practitioner. Both are done at a distance, and either stands on its own.

Hers is the classical way: the body read as a whole rather than the symptom on its own. The order matters. You have to be able to read the whole before you are any use on the part, and that is what takes the years, which is why it is passed on teacher to student, one to one. Illness here is silt in a river and health is the flow: wherever the channel blocks is where symptoms surface, so the work is on the channel. Her hand is light, like wind through a willow.

Who this is for

Three reasons people come to us.

They arrive at different points. Some after years of it, some just starting to look for another way. Any one of these is reason enough.

01

You are on a waiting list

Some people come to us while they wait, wanting a clearer picture of how the whole of them is running. We do not advise on your medical care, and nothing here is a reason to delay, refuse or stop anything your doctor has recommended.

02

Nothing shows on the tests

Years of fatigue, digestion that never settles, sleep that doesn't restore, and a file full of results within normal range. Traditional Chinese Medicine reads the body differently, and asks a different set of questions from the ones your tests answer.

03

Pain, looked at as a whole

Back, neck, shoulder, knee, hip. Whether it started last month or fifteen years ago, she looks at it as part of the whole of you: when it began, what you were doing then, and what the rest of your body has been doing since. Her work in person is hands-on energy work, with gua sha and moxibustion where she judges them right for you. It is not medical treatment.

Sessions in China

Sessions in person are planned from your reading.

We do not sell a fixed programme, because we do not know what you need until you have been read. How long you would come for, and what that costs, is estimated in your Observation Reading and confirmed once she has seen you. Only then is there anything to book.

Sessions are given in person by the same practitioner who writes your reading, an hour a session, rather than as a package. The number of sessions is decided from your reading. For some people that is a few days, for others two or three weeks.

Start with the Observation Reading

What sessions in person involve

  • 01
    You are read first

    The Observation Reading sets out how the practitioner reads your whole picture, what she thinks has shaped it, and what to be careful of. It stands on its own, and most people stop here.

  • 02
    The reading gives an estimate

    If sessions in person would add anything, the report gives her estimate of how many days and what they would cost. The number is confirmed once she has seen you in person.

  • 03
    You decide, with the number in front of you

    If you go ahead, the £850 reading fee is credited in full against your sessions. If you do not, the reading is still yours to keep.

  • 04
    Accompanied where it matters

    An interpreter throughout, and one point of contact in the UK for the whole trip. Flights and accommodation you book yourself, in your own name; we can suggest places near her practice, and we take no commission on any of them.

  • 05
    Afterwards

    A written summary in English, a plan you can follow at home, and scheduled follow-up in the months that follow.

About

Why we exist.

That's what took me to Traditional Chinese Medicine. It asked about the things nobody else had: what I ate, when I slept, how I was living. I've been studying it ever since, sharing what I learn in short videos, and going back to China to shadow senior doctors. YinYang Well is how you get that kind of one-to-one read for yourself.
